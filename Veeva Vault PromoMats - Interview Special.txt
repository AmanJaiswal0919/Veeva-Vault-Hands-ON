1- What is Veeva Vault?

It is a cloud based content management system used by life science companies to manage regulated content, automate review and approval workflow, maintain audit trail version control and ensure compliance with regulatory requirement and provide secure access to content throughout its lifecycle.

2- What is PromoMats?

PromoMats stand for Promotional Material It is used to manage promotional and marketing content

through review and approval workflow to ensure regulatory compliance.
